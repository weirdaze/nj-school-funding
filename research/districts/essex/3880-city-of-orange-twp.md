## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits $7,166,527 → $22,365,135 (+212%): **matches** UFB p. 5, but it is **not a real cost increase**. It reflects benefits shifting between school-based (SBB) budgets and the undistributed line. The 2025-26 revised line ($7.2M) is far below both 2024-25 actual ($13.7M) and audited FY25 health costs ($24.3M). The +$15.2M "benefit increase" and the -$12.2M "gap" should not be used. A rough combined measure, undistributed benefits plus SBB support services, rises from ~$33.4M to ~$40.3M. That measure mixes in non-benefit costs.
- State aid +$3,000,082: **match** UFB total state revenue.
- Levy +4.0%: **match** (base-budget levy $13,023,999 → $13,550,169). The UFB tax-rate page shows a $13,287,084 general fund school levy for Orange City (p. 11), which is unexplained.
- Fund balance $0 → $1,707,931: **match**.

## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** Orange's sample selection rests on an accounting artifact: the 212% benefits jump is a reclassification out of school-based budgets, not a cost spike. The district gets +$3.0M in state aid and raises its levy 4.0%. No public reporting on cuts, layoffs or health costs for 2026-27 was found.

## Confidence
**Low**: the UFB figures reconcile mechanically, but our benefits change is structurally misleading, and no district narrative or news was found.
