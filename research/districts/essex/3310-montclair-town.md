## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits $27,869,184 → $33,166,488 (+19.0%): **match** UFB p. 5. No district figure is available for comparison.
- State aid: our figure is NJDOE formula aid (+$592,625); UFB total state revenue (all state sources) rises +$492,625 (+3.8%), a $100K difference presumably from a non-formula line. The Digest's "6%" matches neither and gives no base; it is consistent only in direction. **Partial match.**
- Levy +3.9%: **match** UFB. This excludes the one-time voter-approved $12.6M deficit levy, which may be levied separately.
- Fund balance $864,310 → $0: **match** UFB p. 2. This is budgeted fund balance (a planned use), not a measure of remaining reserves.

## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** Montclair made deep cuts: over 100 layoff/RIF notices in 2025-26 and the closure of Renaissance Middle School for 2026-27. Reporting attributes them to a ~$18-20M deficit from past overspending and, for the school closure, to declining middle school enrollment, not to benefit costs. Benefits do rise 19% (+$5.3M: $27.87M → $33.17M) in 2026-27, but we found no district statement linking cuts to them. Montclair should not be cited as evidence for the benefits claim.

## Confidence
**Medium**: cuts and their stated cause come from credible local reporting (Patch, opened) plus snippets; our numbers match the UFB, but no district budget document for 2026-27 was opened.
