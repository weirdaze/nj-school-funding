## Assessment
**Confirmed.** Piscataway's own budget book attributes the 44.5 FTE of eliminations (22 instructional, net -23 positions after additions) to "the loss of state aid, reduction in fund balance, and large increases in health care costs." Its presentation names health benefits ($7.6M, +30%) as "the primary driver" and warns that "class sizes and course offerings will be impacted." The district also used a $6.27M health-care waiver (levy +7.7%) and cut two sports and the Jump Ahead program. Reductions are mainly through attrition and vacancies, so actual layoffs are likely far fewer than 44.5.

## Confidence
**High**: all key facts come from the district's budget book, presentation and hearing summary, and they reconcile exactly with our benefits, aid, levy and fund-balance numbers.
