## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits: the UFB shows $41,314,312 → $51,009,128, which matches ours (+23.5%). The original and revised 2025-26 figures are identical in our data, consistent with the UFB.
- Aid: the UFB state revenue change is +$552,410, which matches our UFB figure. Our formula aid change (+$1.42M) is larger, presumably because of the aid categories counted (e.g., the end of $1.0M "Other State Aids").
- Levy: +8.15%, which matches our 8.1%.
- Fund balance: none budgeted in either year, which matches our $0.
- The NJ.com 13.87% figure is the 2025-26 tax-bill increase. The UFB's 2024-25 "actual" levy ($125.56M) against $154.18M implies +22.8%, so the UFB's 2024-25 actual column may not be comparable. This does not affect our 2025-26 → 2026-27 numbers.

## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** Old Bridge has one of the largest benefit increases in the batch (+$9.7M, +23.5%) under the SEHBP. It has no reserves left, and the levy rises another 8.1% on top of the 2025-26 increase, while regular-instruction spending falls $1.8M. However, no 2026-27 district document or news report was reachable to show whether that reflects position cuts or what the district attributes it to. The 2025-26 reporting ties prior cuts to state aid, not benefits.

## Confidence
