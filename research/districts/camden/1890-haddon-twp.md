## Assessment
**Consistent.** Haddon Township cut about 5+ positions: an unreplaced HS math teacher and registrar, one elementary teacher, three 1:1 aides, and daily substitute coverage. Reporting ties these cuts to a gap driven partly by a 25%, later 18%, health increase. They came on top of a third straight year of state-aid loss, and with no surplus left to draw. The district also blames state aid ("Solutions must come from Trenton"), so health costs are one of two named drivers. Our 2025-26 benefits base ($8,725,427) is higher than the UFB "revised" figure ($8,403,537). Measured against the revised figure, the increase is about +17.4%, not +13.0%.
