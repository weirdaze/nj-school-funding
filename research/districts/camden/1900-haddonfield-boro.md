Keeping current status would require an 8.16% levy, "beyond the district's legal taxing authority." It warns that staff reductions would affect electives and "Class sizes. ## Assessment
**Confirmed.** Haddonfield's own materials name the benefits increase (+$1.8M; health benefits +$1.575M, the largest single line) as the core of a $3.45M gap. They explicitly warn of larger MS/HS class sizes. The adopted plan cut 3.5 FTE teachers, 5 paras/EAs, part-time central office staff, a maintenance position, stipends, and coaching. It also needed a health-care cap adjustment ($1.3M) and banked cap ($326K) to reach a roughly 6% levy. One discrepancy: our aid change is -$50,958, while the district describes +$49,042 versus 2025-26, so the 2025-26 aid base differs, probably in which aid lines are counted. Our 2025-26 benefits base ($9,175,472) is also higher than the UFB "revised" figure ($8,871,541); against the revised figure the increase is +20.2%.
