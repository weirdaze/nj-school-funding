## Assessment
**Consistent.** Deep cuts (34 positions) and explicitly larger class sizes are confirmed, but the board attributes the crunch primarily to Hammonton sending-receiving tuition and a collapsing surplus; benefits growth is mentioned as a contributor. The reported "19.8%" benefit increase conflicts with both our figure (+2.0%) and the state UFB (+3.3%) — possibly a health-premium rate rather than the budget line, or the line shrank because of the staff cuts.
