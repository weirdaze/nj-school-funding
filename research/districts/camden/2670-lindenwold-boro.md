## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Lindenwold's benefits jumped $2.5M (+22%), far more than its $736K aid increase, and the district used a health-care cap adjustment (+1%). Even so, the budget adds 17 FTE with no reductions listed. Lindenwold is a high-aid district (83% state aid) that also draws large capital reserves, and benefit growth did not force visible cuts this year. Our figures match the district's deck exactly.
