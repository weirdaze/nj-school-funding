## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** State aid rose about $2.6M, which far exceeds the benefit-line increase. The levy rose only the 2% cap, and no cuts were reported. The jump in benefits as a share of salaries (35.9% → 42.2%) still shows rising health costs, but here they were absorbed by aid growth.

**Data caveat:** Our 71260 benefits figure ($2.76M) is much smaller than audited FY25 health spending ($5.67M). Most benefits are probably budgeted inside school-based budgets, so the 11.7% "benefits share" and the +10.3% growth likely understate this district's real benefit costs.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the district's UFB verifies aid growth, the 2% levy and SHIF membership, but "no cuts" is inferred from budget lines, and our 71260 benefits figure does not capture benefits budgeted inside school-based budgets.
