## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Eastern lost $277K in state aid (the district's figure, which matches our NJDOE formula aid change of −$0.28M) while benefits rose about $1.06M ($8.76M − $7.70M, +13.7%), and the district reports an 18.70% healthcare increase (slide 22). Even so, the district's own presentation says it keeps all programs and adds courses, funding the gap with a 4.14% levy increase (above 2% using part of the 3.5% healthcare adjustment and 0.28% banked cap shown on slide 19; dollar amounts not given) and a larger capital-reserve draw. On discrepancies:
- Our 2026-27 fund balance figure ($1,810,654) differs from the tentative presentation, which shows $1,782,296 excess surplus plus $924,366 unassigned. Our 2025-26 figure ($1,903,985) matches only the excess-surplus line.
- Audited health growth was low (4.3% FY23→FY25), yet budgeted benefits rose 13.7%; the district's 18.70% healthcare increase (slide 22) explains the jump.

## Confidence
**High**: the district's own budget presentation confirms programs and staffing are kept, the $277K aid cut and the 4.14% levy; our aid figure (-$0.28M) matches; the minor fund-balance difference (tentative vs final budget) is noted in the Assessment.
