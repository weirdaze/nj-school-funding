## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Magnolia's benefits growth (+$190K) was roughly matched by state aid growth (+$214K). The district used enrollment and banked-cap adjustments to raise its levy 6.7%. The banked-cap resolution explicitly cites "staff salaries and benefit costs," and the budget adds instruction spending rather than cutting it. The real pressure is the very high benefits-to-salary ratio (about 48%) and heavy reliance on fund balance ($2.04M), not cuts this year. Our figures match the UFB and the adoption resolution.
