## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Benefits jumped 22.8% (+$4.4M), and the district absorbed it almost entirely through a $4.22M health-care cap adjustment plus banked cap, producing an 11.6% levy increase — the cost was shifted to taxpayers rather than to staffing or programs. Our figures match the presentation. Our list marks Winslow as an SEHBP participant, but the Inquirer's South Jersey SEHBP list omits it — verify.
