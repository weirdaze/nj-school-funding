## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district raised its levy 6.93%, cut 10 positions (3 full-time and 7 part-time), and raised student fees to close a roughly $2M gap. Local reporting ties that gap to health-care costs, including GLP-1 drugs and about $1.2M in added health expense, alongside flat or declining state aid. The board's own resolution blames health premiums and the aid distribution, but no district document seen directly attributes specific cuts to health costs. The June article's "preserved staff" conflicts with the September report of 10 positions cut, which may reflect the March versus May budget versions. Our figures (levy +6.9%, benefits +15.6%, aid -$45K) agree with the reporting.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the 10 positions cut, 6.93% levy and ~$1.2M health increase are verified in local news (The Retrospect, three articles), and our levy and benefits figures agree, but no district document confirming the cuts was read and the March "preserved staff" report conflicts with the later cuts.
