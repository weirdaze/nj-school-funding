## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district's own analysis lists the 20.3% health-benefit increase (+$204,083) as one of three factors that made cuts ($156,600 in line items; one FTE eliminated per The Retrospect) necessary despite higher state aid. The largest factor is the loss of $303,722 in one-time fund balance/reserve support, which the analysis says "explains why increased state aid did not eliminate the need for cuts"; shared services (+$69,592) is the third. Health is therefore a named factor but not the main one. District figures closely match ours on benefits (+$204,083 vs our +$0.21M, +19.6%) and levy (+7.0%). The district's "state aid" (+$126,254, total state aid of $3,131,687) is a broader figure than our NJDOE formula aid (+$0.15M), so the two are not directly comparable.

## Confidence
