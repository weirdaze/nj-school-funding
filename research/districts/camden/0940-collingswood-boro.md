## Assessment
**Consistent.** Collingswood closed an elementary school, eliminated 33 positions/programs, added fees and expects larger elementary classes, with a 22% health insurance increase and a state aid cut both cited as drivers of the $3.3M gap — but the district frames it as a long-running revenue/structural problem rather than benefits alone. Discrepancies: reported aid cut $305K vs ours -$501K; reported 22% health increase vs our +12.8% benefits line — partly because the 2025-26 benefits budget ($8.95M, matching the Retrospect's "anticipated" figure) was well above actual spending ($7.33M), so budget-to-budget growth understates growth from actuals. Audited health growth FY23→FY25 was only +3.3%.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the cuts, 22% health increase and aid cut are verified in credible local news (NBC10, Inquirer, Retrospect), but no district document was checked and our aid and benefits figures only partly reconcile.
