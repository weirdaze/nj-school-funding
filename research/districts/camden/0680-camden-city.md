## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence** (reclassified from Consistent on fact check; see below). Substantial layoffs (≈100 central-office plus 46 more including 23 teachers) occurred, and officials cite "rising costs," but none of the reporting names health benefits; the stated drivers are enrollment loss to charters/Renaissance schools and shrinking federal aid. Our numbers show state aid +$21.4M vs benefits +$1.56M, so the benefit-cost explanation fits poorly here; however, audited health spending grew +59.8% FY23→FY25 and the budgeted 2026-27 benefits line ($18.3M) is well below FY25 audited health alone ($24.8M), suggesting the budget line may be understated or costs are charged elsewhere (e.g., grants). Reported aid increase ($23M) is close to our $21.4M.

## Confidence
**Medium**: layoffs and stated causes verified in three credible news stories; none names health benefits, and our budgeted benefits line fell, so the claim is not supported here.
