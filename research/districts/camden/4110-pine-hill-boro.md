## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** The district explicitly identifies a 19.3% health premium increase ($1.47M) as a major cost driver and used a $624,887 health-care cap adjustment, but it closed the gap with supply reductions, staffing "strategy," and the levy increase, and it added K-5 support staff and classrooms. Biggest structural issue is a 48% drop in budgeted fund balance. District state aid change (+$1,680,918) differs from ours (+$1,970,918) by exactly $290,000; district health increase ($1.47M) exceeds our total benefit-line increase (+$1.07M), suggesting offsets elsewhere in benefits.
