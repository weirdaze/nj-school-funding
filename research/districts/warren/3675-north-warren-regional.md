## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits: the district's "$3.54M" matches the UFB's $3,541,784 and ours. The 2025-26 revised figure is $2,852,394, so the change is +$689,390 (+24.2%), which matches.
- Levy: +4.17% per the district, which matches ours (4.2%). A 2% increase would give $14,755,490, so roughly **$314,538** above 2% comes from adjustments. The district says this includes the healthcare adjustment. This is derived by us; the district did not state the amount.
- State aid: the UFB shows total state sources up $25,179, which matches our state_revenue_change_ufb. Our formula aid figure is +$75,179. The $50K difference likely reflects non-formula state revenue in the UFB (not verified).
- Fund balance: $825,000 and $900,290 match.
