## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** The district's own UFB confirms a large jump in benefits ($6.29M → $7.24M; benefits rising to 45% of salaries) while K-12 formula aid fell $0.45M and the county levy was flat. With enrollment flat at 1,646, the UFB shows regular instruction, special-ed instruction, cocurricular, child study team and equipment lines all falling, total general current expense down, and an implied salary base about 4% smaller. That pattern is consistent with staff or program reductions, but no district document or reporting describes cuts or links them to benefit costs, so the classification stays Insufficient evidence. Budgeted fund balance is $2.5M (down from $3.0M).
