## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Logan explicitly reports no teaching cuts and adds two teaching positions (K and grade 6), while grade 7 drops from four sections to three and overall enrollment declines 1.9%. The district's +$237,107 state aid increase reconciles with our formula aid (+$93,751) once preschool aid (+$329,580) and the loss of tax incentive aid (-$186,224) are counted; -$92,473 is the UFB general fund state revenue change, not formula aid.
