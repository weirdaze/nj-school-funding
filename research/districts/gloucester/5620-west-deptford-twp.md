## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district states that health costs required a $637,131 cap adjustment. It cut instructional and other line items and consolidated positions through attrition while benefits grew by about $2.2M. It explicitly says these steps were taken "rather than making cuts," with "minimal impact on students and class sizes." So the evidence shows benefit-driven belt-tightening and attrition, not layoffs or larger classes.

Discrepancy: the district's "Employee Benefits" figure for 2026-27 is $13,098,223 (+19.9%), versus our $13,513,723 (+23.7%). The 2025-26 base matches ($10,923,175), so the gap ($415,500) is in the 2026-27 figure. It may come from a different category mapping or from the advertised versus final budget.
