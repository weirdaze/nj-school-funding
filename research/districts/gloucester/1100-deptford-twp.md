## Assessment
**Consistent** (based on the 2025-26 cycle). In 2025-26 Deptford cut 13 certificated positions, 12 paraprofessionals and others (8 RIFs), and listed a $2.8M healthcare premium increase as the largest single cost driver alongside salaries, transportation, grant losses and fund balance decline. For 2026-27, despite our largest benefit jump in this batch (+$4.14M vs. +$380K aid; corrected 2026-10-24 from "+$161K", see Fact check), no cuts were reported. The local report lists new positions. The gap appears to be funded by more fund balance, a levy held at 2%, and line shifts; the special education and basic skills instruction lines fall, but this is unexplained. The "$1,000,000 health care adjustment" reported for 2026-27 does not show in the 2.0% UFB levy change. It may conflate the 2025-26 $1M cap-adjustment language, or it may be a different item, so verify.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the 2025-26 cuts and the 15%/21% health premium increase are verified in the district's own presentation and 2026-27 UFB figures match ours, but the classification rests on the prior budget cycle and the 2026-27 picture (no cuts, new positions, "$1M health adjustment") rests on one local newsletter.
