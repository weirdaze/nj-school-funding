## Assessment
Kingsway explicitly identifies health benefits as a major cost driver: about $1.4M, +15.8% SHIF renewal, and a $1.3M health-benefit cap waiver that drives most of the 6.72% levy increase. The same budget cuts 9.0 FTE, mostly in administration, alongside a 38-student enrollment decline. The district does not attribute the FTE cut to benefits; it frames it with enrollment. Classification: **Consistent**. The district's figures broadly match ours: benefits +15% / +$1.5M versus our +15.1% / +$1.58M, and fund balance $346,800. The state aid change differs depending on the document: -$392,506 in the presentation versus -$307,506 in the Budget in Brief table. Ours (-$307,506) matches the latter.
