## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** Harrison faces a large benefit jump (+30.5%, +$1.31M = $5.60M − $4.29M in our numbers; benefits rising to 36.8% of salaries) on top of a 3% cut in formula aid (-$158,040, matching the district's figure exactly; the UFB total state revenue change is -$100,999). Its budget trims the technology base and eliminates unfilled positions on the position control roster. The district says those positions "are NOT staff members"; it does not say whether any current employee was cut, and gives no count. It does not tie these trims to health costs; it frames them as "to balance this budget". (Reclassified from Consistent after adversarial review: the district never names benefits as a cause of the trims, so the link rests only on our numbers.)

## Confidence
**Medium**: the trims (unfilled positions, technology base) and the 3% aid cut are verified in the district's own hearing presentation and our formula aid matches the district's figure, but no source connects the trims to health costs.
