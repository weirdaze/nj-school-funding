## Assessment
**Consistent** (mild). Gateway lists rising salaries and benefits combined ($1.49M; benefits are not broken out) as the cause of a $628K gap. It closed the gap partly by eliminating three positions that had been unfilled since 2024-25 (aide, teacher, supervisor; $229K) and $149K in other appropriation reductions. No current staff were laid off: the district raised the levy 0.8% above the cap "to maintain all current staffing levels," and programs were kept, helped by a large aid increase (+$694K formula aid, matching the district's figure exactly). Benefits rise +20.7% (+$1.07M: $5.15M → $6.22M) on the UFB revised base, matching Our numbers.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the $628K gap, the salaries-and-benefits driver and the three eliminated (unfilled) positions are verified in the district's own presentation and its aid figure matches ours exactly, but the driver combines salaries with benefits, its two slides give different totals ($1,260,450 vs $1,489,450), and the cuts are vacancies.
