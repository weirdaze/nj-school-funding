## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Jackson used a $7.31M health care cap adjustment, nearly the whole $7.73M benefits increase, explicitly "to continue the educational programs ... at its current level". That drove an 8.8% levy increase. It closed a ~$10M gap with a $10M fund-balance draw backed by school-sale proceeds, rather than with reported 2026-27 layoffs. The district says that approach is unsustainable and is seeking a $7.8M special question in December 2026.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the health adjustment, levy and gap come from the district's own resolutions and web page and reconcile exactly with our numbers. But no 2026-27 budget presentation or news coverage was found, and the personnel agenda pages were reviewed only partly, so "no cuts" is inferred.
