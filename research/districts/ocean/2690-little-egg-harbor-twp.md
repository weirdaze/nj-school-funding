## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Little Egg Harbor reported a 32.7% health premium increase but held the levy to 2% with no health care adjustment, and its budget adds 7 certificated positions. The benefits line fell 12.3% after a "reduction in health benefits" the district did not explain. If that means a cut in employee coverage, that would be a cost shift rather than a staff or program cut.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the key facts come from the district's own final budget presentation and agendas, and the benefits and levy figures reconcile. But the drop in the benefits line despite a 32.7% premium increase is unexplained, and the district's 2025-26 aid figure differs from the UFB's.

## Open questions
- What is the "reduction in health benefits"? A plan or carrier change, or lower coverage?
- Why was the 2025-26 benefits budget revised to $8.97M, up 44% on the 2024-25 actual of $6.21M?
- Why did the 2025-26 levy rise about 33% ($14.6M to $19.4M)? Was it an S-2 adjustment or a referendum?
