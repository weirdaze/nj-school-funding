## Assessment
**Confirmed.** In its own hearing presentation, the district names "out of control health insurance" costs (+25%, +$648,080), alongside state aid losses and utilities, as the reason "further cuts were necessary," and lists 11 positions cut, including teachers and paraprofessionals. Health costs are one of several drivers (state and preschool aid loss and the lost banked cap are about as large combined), and the district had already left SEHBP for SCHIF to soften the increase.

## Confidence
**High**: key claims come from the district's own Apr. 30, 2026 hearing presentation, corroborated by two local news reports, and our benefits, aid, levy and fund-balance figures reconcile exactly.
