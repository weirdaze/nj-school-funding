## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district cut 11 positions (four by resignation or retirement) and lists "out of control health insurance and utility costs" alongside "loss of state funding" as reasons (slide 12). But the +$648,080 health increase is matched one-for-one by a +$648,080 health-care levy adjustment (slides 10-11), which "only pays for health care, not student services" (slide 15). The remaining gap comes mainly from the other items the district lists: lost state aid (-$63,037), lost banked cap (-$170,951), lost preschool aid (-$227,645; together $461,633), reduced fund balance, salaries and utilities, set against long-run enrollment decline (K-8 enrollment 1,092 in 2010 → 580 in 2025) and S2 aid losses. Health is a stated contributor, not the stated cause. (Reclassified from Confirmed after adversarial review.)
