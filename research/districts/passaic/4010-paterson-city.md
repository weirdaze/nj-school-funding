But the UFB shows 2024-25 actual benefits of $57.23M, and our audited FY25 health figure is $73.0M, so the budget line is incomplete as a measure of Paterson's health costs (flag `benefits_line_incomplete`=True). - Levy: UFB $79.29M → $85.69M = +8.1%, matching ours and reported "8%".

## Assessment
**Consistent.** Paterson cut more than 200 positions and laid off ~39-43 teachers plus ~50 other staff, and closed four schools. The superintendent named healthcare as one of several cost drivers, along with special education, transportation, loss of federal relief, the end of one-shot revenue and charter growth. Unlike most districts, Paterson got a large aid increase (+$37M), but most of it went to charter payments. The district has not published a health-cost figure that we found.

## Confidence
**Medium**: cuts and the health mention come from consistent local TV/news reports, and the UFB reconciles with our aid, levy and fund-balance numbers. But no district budget presentation was opened, and our benefits line does not capture Paterson's full health cost.
