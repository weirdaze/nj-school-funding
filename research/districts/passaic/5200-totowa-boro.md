## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits: UFB $5,418,109 → $7,466,039 (+37.8%) matches ours. Caveat: the 2025-26 budget base is far above actual spending. Benefits were $3.28M actual in 2024-25 and audited FY25 health was $2.66M, while total current expense jumped 61% from 2024-25 actual to 2025-26 budget. The district appears to budget large amounts funded by reserves, so the +$2.05M budget-to-budget benefits increase may overstate the real cost pressure.

- Levy: +3.81% per presentation (slide 15) and UFB, matching ours (3.8%).
- Fund balance: $7.42M → $9.78M per UFB, matching ours. The draw comes from a large restricted reserve rather than unassigned surplus.

## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Totowa's own presentation lists no reductions and says the goal is to maintain class sizes and programs. Its 3.81% levy increase and $9.8M reserve draw cover the budget. The budgeted benefits jump is large in percentage terms but sits on a budget base well above actual spending, and the district does not discuss health costs publicly.

## Confidence
**Medium**: based on the district's own presentation, minutes and UFB, which reconcile with our numbers. But the presentation is silent on benefits, and we found no news coverage to confirm there were no staffing changes.
