## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district calls health insurance its "major challenge." It used a $3.29M medical cap adjustment plus $1.06M of banked cap to fund a +15.1% ($3.8M) health increase, and it lists more than $1M of reductions over 24 months, mostly supervisory and administrative roles plus one elementary Spanish teacher. Those reductions span two budget years and are not tied specifically to health costs. The 2026-27 budget's stated goal was to avoid "impacting instructional programs or services," so the main response in 2026-27 was a large levy increase, not layoffs.
