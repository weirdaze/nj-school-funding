## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Union Beach left SEHBP for the SHIF in 2025-26. For 2026-27 it used a $346,115 health care cap adjustment, which accounts for about 3 points of the 4.9% levy increase. The spring 2026 board agendas show no RIFs or eliminated positions. The district's documented staff cuts came earlier and are attributed to S-2 state aid reductions.

## Confidence
