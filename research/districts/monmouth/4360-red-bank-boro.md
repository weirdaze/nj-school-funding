## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Red Bank's benefits line grew 25% because of a 30% premium hike. The district says outright that it used the $1.64M health care cap adjustment, covering about 75% of the $2.2M increase, to *avoid* cuts to "staff, programs and class sizes". It added a 4th-grade section rather than cutting one. The cost was passed to taxpayers through a 9.52% levy increase, the second year in a row above 9%.

## Confidence
**High**: the key facts come from the district's own budget hearing presentation and the UFB, and our benefits, aid, levy and fund balance figures reconcile exactly. The only gap ($2.2M versus $1.82M) has a plausible explanation.
