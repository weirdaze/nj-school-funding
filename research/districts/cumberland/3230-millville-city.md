## Verification of our numbers
- Benefits $8,887,188 → $8,256,281 (-7.1%): matches UFB p. 4 mechanically, but **not a valid measure**: the line is **incomplete**. Benefits as a share of salaries rise sharply (44.16% → 50.09%), and SBB appropriations rise 8.4%. Part of the benefit cost is budgeted in school-based budgets, so the -7.1% almost certainly understates benefit growth.
- State aid change +$5,116,570: **match** to UFB total state revenue (p. 2).
- Levy +6.0%: **match** to UFB p. 2.
- Fund balance used $1,100,000 → $1,300,000: **match** to UFB p. 2.

**Insufficient evidence.** Millville gets a large aid increase (+$5.1M) and still raises the levy 6.0%.
