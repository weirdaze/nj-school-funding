## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** Willingboro explicitly identifies health benefits as a top cost driver (+$3.8M, about 33%, on the deck's health line; 31.9% medical / 22% Rx rate estimates on p. 11, though p. 19 cites a 14% premium increase). That +$3.8M is measured from the district's original 2025-26 budget; the 2025-26 benefits line was revised up mid-year, and on the UFB revised base total benefits rise only 5.0% ($16.65M → $17.48M). The district absorbed the increase with a $2.34M health-benefits cap adjustment and $1.8M banked cap (levy +13%), alongside a +$3.48M NJDOE formula aid increase (our figure), while adding counselors and programs; only unspecified "attrition" is mentioned. The deck's 2025-26 benefits total ($14,599,576) is close to the UFB original budget ($14.64M), but the deck's 2026-27 total ($18.6M) is above the UFB's $17.48M, and the deck's $157K budgeted fund balance conflicts with the UFB's $3.0M.

## Confidence
**Medium**: the UFB and district deck figures are verified, but the deck's 2026-27 benefits and fund-balance figures do not reconcile with the UFB, and the deck gives two different premium increases (31.9% and 14%).
