## Assessment
**Consistent.** The district explicitly ties the levy increase to a $5.9M health-benefit increase, and it eliminated about eight positions by attrition and started a subcontracting notice for aides; but the cuts were modest and the main response was a 26% levy increase (plus banked cap and $12.1M of fund balance), not layoffs. Big discrepancy: the district's $5.9M health increase is ~4x our budget-line benefits increase ($1.5M), and our 2025-26 base ($10.4M) differs from the UFB revised figure ($9.7M); the FY25 audited health figure ($13.7M) exceeds the whole 2026-27 benefits line, so much of Pemberton's health cost is likely charged outside line 71260 (e.g., to salary-function or grant accounts).
