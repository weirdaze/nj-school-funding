## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence** (no sign of benefit-driven cuts). Budgeted benefits are flat (+0.2%), aid rose more than benefits, regular instruction grew 4.3% with enrollment up about 6%, and the levy rose only 2%. The district has nearly stopped using fund balance ($1.42M → $0.07M), which suggests the large 2025-26 levy increase rebuilt its structural position. Nothing suggests benefit costs forced cuts here; flat budgeted benefits against a ~15-30% market premium environment is notable and may reflect a plan change or an over-budgeted 2025-26 base.
