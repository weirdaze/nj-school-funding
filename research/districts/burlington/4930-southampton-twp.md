## Assessment
**No cuts reported.** The district itself cites a 16-17% health-benefit increase as a top 2026-27 obstacle, but the minutes record no cuts. The business administrator says the increase is budgeted, and the levy rose only 2%. Our benefits line shows only +1.2%, which conflicts with the district's 16-17% premium increase. Either 2025-26 was over-budgeted (the 2024-25 actual was $2.08M) or costs were offset elsewhere, so the increase may have been absorbed by an inflated 2025-26 base, even while the district used $0.5M less fund balance.

## Confidence
**Medium**: district minutes verify the 16-17% health increase, but our budget-line increase (+1.2%) does not reconcile with it, and the budget presentation is not posted.
