## Assessment
**No cuts reported** for 2026-27. The district explicitly says there are no staff or program reductions, despite a $4.6M (16.8%) health increase that it attributes partly to Chapter 44. The large deep cuts happened in 2024-25 and were tied to state aid loss, not benefits. There is a discrepancy in the aid figure: the district reports +$1,101,830 (SFRA aid, +6%), while our number is +$601,830 (total UFB general-fund state revenue, which likely includes a lower extraordinary-aid estimate). The district's health increase ($4.62M) is also larger than our net benefits-line increase ($3.66M).
