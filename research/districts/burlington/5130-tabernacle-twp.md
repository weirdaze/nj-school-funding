**Consistent** (strongest case in this batch). Tabernacle cut 7-8 positions and eliminated most sports/extracurriculars, with likely larger classes. Its own budget deck lists health (+15.35%, $434,853) and Rx (+24%, $177,146) as the two largest new costs, about $612K, which is twice the salary increase. Board members, though, mainly blame state aid cuts and below-adequacy funding, so benefits are a major factor among several. Our +6.4% benefits figure uses the 2025-26 *original* base ($3.82M); against the UFB 2025-26 revised figure ($3.64M) the increase is +11.5%. Benefits are 59% of salaries in the UFB, the highest ratio in this batch.
