## Assessment
**Confirmed.** The district explicitly presents the health benefits increase (~$4.1M, "30-40%") against a $1.78M cap-limited levy increase. It closes the gap by eliminating 7 elementary teaching positions (about 1 per elementary school), 1 elementary AP, and late busing, which curtails elementary extracurriculars, with class sizes projected up to 26–27 in grades 3–5. It also cites long-running S2 aid losses, so benefits are the immediate driver on top of a structural aid shortfall. Discrepancies:
- **Benefits baseline:** our 2025-26 benefits ($18.16M) appears to be the original budget, while the UFB's 2025-26 revised figure is $21.12M. Measured from revised, the increase is about +20% ($4.2M), which matches the district's +$4.2M.
- **State aid:** our -$736,932 differs from the district's +$429,594 in formula and categorical aid. The district separately shows the loss of $710,602 in one-time Tax Levy Incentive Aid.
