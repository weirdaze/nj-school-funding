## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** No cuts were found in public reporting. The +65.7% benefits jump is probably not a like-for-like health premium increase. The general fund benefits line covers only a fraction of audited health costs, and grant and special-revenue funding is shrinking, so the jump likely reflects benefits moving from grants back into the general fund (plus premium growth). The district leaned heavily on reserves: about $5.9M of fund balance and a 6.5% levy increase.

## Confidence
**Low**: insufficient evidence; UFB figures verify, but there is no district narrative and the benefits jump is not reconciled with audited health costs.
