## Assessment
**Insufficient evidence.** The levy is held to 2% while benefits rise 8.9%. The district leans on fund balance ($2.0M) and per-pupil classroom spending dips as enrollment grows, but no public statement of cuts was found. The +17.8% levy jump in 2025-26 is unexplained (possibly a levy adjustment or referendum) and should be checked.

## Confidence
**Low**: the UFB figures are verified, but there is no district statement on cuts or health costs, and the Assessment's +8.9% understates the verified increase (+12.3%).
