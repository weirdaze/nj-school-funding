## Assessment
**Consistent.** Dover eliminated an unspecified number of elementary, middle and high school teaching positions plus two administrative posts (~$1.1M). It lists a $4.9M (28%) benefits increase driven by 29.8% rate hikes as the top budget pressure, alongside a $1.88M state aid cut. The presentation frames the cuts as "efficiency" rather than tying them directly to health costs. The district used $2M of an available $3.8M health care adjustment, which brought the levy increase to 11%.

## Confidence
**High**: all key facts come from the district's own budget presentation, and our benefits, aid, levy and fund balance figures reconcile exactly with it and the UFB. The only gap is that no position counts were given.
